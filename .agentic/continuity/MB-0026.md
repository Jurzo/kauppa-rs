## Decisions and constraints

- RR-0013 is the contract; nothing outside it was decided beyond `may_decide` (data structure, test names) and these details:
  - the limiter lives under `routes/auth/limiter/` so it stays a security trigger path;
  - "N minutes" uses the singular for 1 ("Try again in 1 minute.");
  - the register 429 page also keeps the typed display name and email (never the password), as the duplicate-email page does;
  - register counts every client-error answer (4xx) as a failure; server errors and successes don't count;
  - the trusted-proxy fallback WARN fires once per `AuthLimiter` (one per process in `main.rs`); the capacity WARN carries `limiter` and `key_kind`, no key;
  - an unparsable `X-Forwarded-For` entry met before an untrusted one (walking from the right) means fallback to the proxy's bucket.
- RR-0013 constraint 7 ("no existing test needs changing") did not hold after MB-0023: `register_requires_invite_same_answer_for_every_invalid_token` sends 16 failures from the unknown-peer bucket. Each request now carries its own peer address; its assertions are unchanged.
