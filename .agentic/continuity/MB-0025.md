## Decisions and constraints

- Tests use temp-file databases under `std::env::temp_dir()` with a uuid name, removed on drop (with `-wal`/`-shm`/`-journal`). `tempfile` is only a transitive dependency; adding it as a dev-dependency would be a dependency change, so it wasn't.
