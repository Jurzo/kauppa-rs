## Dead ends and rejected approaches

- `default_service(web::to(...))` for unknown URLs: not needed (the scope's
  not-found already passes its middleware), and `csrf::tests::coverage`
  forbids `web::to(`.
- Rewrite every error answer that isn't `text/html`: turns `/healthz`'s
  `text/plain` 503 into a page.
- Holding `req.request().clone()` across `next.call`: panics in actix-web.
