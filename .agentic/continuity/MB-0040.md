## Decisions and constraints

- 2026-11-09, owner: brief accepted. Pattern: native **`popover`** panel opened by a menu button (`popovertarget`) on phones; Escape and outside tap (light dismiss) close it; no JavaScript. Choosing an entry navigates (hx-boost swaps `<body>`), which leaves the panel closed. Sign out stays a POST form (MB-0018) inside the panel.
