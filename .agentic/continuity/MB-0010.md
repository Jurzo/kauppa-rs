## Dead ends and rejected approaches

- `NOCASE`, `lower()` expression indexes, and generated columns are ASCII-only.
- A custom per-connection collation breaks the index for other tools.
- An app-only check without an index is racy.
- Putting the P4/P5/P7 tests in a `repo::name_key::tests` submodule: the brief's `repo::name_key::<name>` filters wouldn't select them. They are at module level instead.

## Decisions and constraints

- Decision: a Rust-computed `name_key` with a unique index on `(owner_id, name_key)`. Rationale: Unicode-aware, and the name stays as typed. Resolution Record: RR-0003 (amends RR-0001).
- Decision (implementer, `may_decide`): the helper lives in `crates/kauppa-core/src/repo/name_key.rs`; the Conflict message echoes the typed name.
- Constraint discovered: "Oldest" compares `created_at` as TEXT. That is correct for sqlx's chrono RFC3339 encoding (UTC `+00:00`; trailing-zero fraction trimming sorts correctly because `+` < digits).
- Constraint discovered: Email case is a separate, security-tier decision: kauppa-web lowercases emails server-side.
