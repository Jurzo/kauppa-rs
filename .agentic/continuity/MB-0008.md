## Decisions and constraints

- Decision: share with an existing user by email, with view/edit roles, through one central authorization predicate. Resolution Record: RR-0002.
- Decisions under `may_decide`:
  - Role is a `TEXT` column with `CHECK (role IN ('view','edit'))`, not a lookup table. Table follows the brief's plan: `id`, `list_id`, `grantee_id`, `role`, `created_at`, `UNIQUE(list_id, grantee_id)`, both FKs `ON DELETE CASCADE`, plus an index on `(grantee_id, list_id)`.
  - Share methods live on `ShoppingListRepo`, so the predicate stays private to one impl.
  - Writes authorize inside `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`; reads inside a read transaction.
  - Owner-only: `rename`, `delete`, `shares`/`share`/`revoke`, and the catalog-reading `add_product`, `add_recipe`, `assign_category`. `edit`: `add_item`, `set_checked`, `remove_item`, `restore_item`. `view`: `get`, `access`, `items`, `store_view`.
  - Share-by-email matches ignoring ASCII case (`lower(email) = lower(?)`), grants only when exactly one account other than the owner matches; re-sharing changes the role in place.
  - UI: a separate owner-only `/lists/{id}/sharing` page; "Shared with you" section on the index.
