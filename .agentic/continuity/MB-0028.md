## Decisions and constraints

- `may_decide` (how the burst is driven, local mode only), after reading `routes/auth/limiter/mod.rs` and `routes/auth/mod.rs`: a login is checked (client limit 10, then account limit 5, both per 900 s) before any work; a throttled 429 is not counted; a 401 counts against both. The section runs right after the app is restarted (`compose start kauppa` at the end of the echo part), so the windows are empty and the counts are exact:
  - (a) 11 failed logins for one nonexistent account, each spoofing `X-Forwarded-For` from its own /24 (198.18.i.7): expect 401 ×5 then 429 ×6, with `Retry-After` and "Too many attempts. Try again in" on the last.
  - (b) 6 failed logins for 6 fresh accounts, new spoofs: expect 401 ×5 then 429. (a) left 5 failures on the client; only the per-client limit can answer here, so all-401 would mean the spoof changed the key.
  - The throttle WARN lines (`auth attempt throttled`, `key_kind` account and client) all carry one `http.client_ip` (the limiter key cut to /24, `telemetry.rs`). It must equal the /24 of the address the echo upstream saw in `X-Forwarded-For`, and be neither a spoofed 198.18.x nor the `internal` subnet. If it were the `internal` subnet, Caddy wasn't trusted and everyone would share one bucket, which (b) alone can't tell apart.
  - No `forwarded-for missing from a trusted proxy` in the app container's logs (both processes of the run), and the logs must be non-empty.
- The static check compares strings: one uncommented `KAUPPA_TRUSTED_PROXIES` line and one subnet under `networks: internal:` must match exactly (quotes stripped), so a wider range or a second entry fails.
