## Decisions and constraints

- RR-0049 (approved 2026-11-16) is the authority for the implementation: its ten sub-answers and seven constraints. If the hand-written `MessageBody` doesn't work, a `deploy/` change is needed, or the edit sections can't be kept with `hx-preserve`, the implementation stops and asks.
- Implementation choices, inside RR-0049 (none renames an event, the URL or a brief test):
  - `Retry-After` is 30 seconds (`RETRY_AFTER_SECS` in `routes/lists/live.rs`); RR-0049 names the header, not the value.
  - A connect during shutdown is refused with the same 503.
  - If the re-check fails with a database error (not an answer about access), the stream ends without `stop` and logs ERROR; the page reconnects and the connect checks again.
  - The catch-up mark is `<process epoch>.<sequence>` in `?since=`; an absent, malformed or older-process mark gets one `changed` at once. A reconnect reuses the page's mark, so it catches up whenever the list changed since the page was rendered.
  - The edit section's key is a `DefaultHasher` digest of the saved quantity and category id, stable within one build; after a deploy an open section may be replaced once.
  - The live re-fetch is recognised by `HX-Target` (`list-items-{id}`, `shop-{id}`), and says which forms its page shows with `?shown=` (`edit|read`-`owner|shared`-`active|archived`). Without `shown` the forms part is sent.
  - The live answers repeat the layout's title suffix (` — kauppalista.app`); `rename_reaches_heading_and_title` compares it with the page's.
  - A user's connect makes their other streams write a keep-alive comment at once (see the decision above).
  - The share route announces whenever the share request is accepted, also when no account matched (nothing is told to the owner; a viewer's page re-fetches once).
  - The browser harness has a second account (RR-0041 doesn't limit accounts); the flows stay as strict, except the reconnect flow's allowance.
- Side effects of `hx-preserve` (owner's Q7 A):
  - the viewer's own submissions keep other items' open edit sections too (before, every swap closed them);
  - saving an item with nothing changed leaves its section open;
  - a kept section shows the category picker as it was rendered; after the owner renames or reorders categories it is stale until that section is replaced;
  - without `Element.moveBefore` the kept section loses focus (its text and open state stay).
- Older RRs that bind the design, checked for CRP-0049: RR-0002 (constraints 1, 4, 5), RR-0010, RR-0013, RR-0014, RR-0015, RR-0017, RR-0018, RR-0019, RR-0025, RR-0026, RR-0029, RR-0036, RR-0038, RR-0041, RR-0045, RR-0048.
