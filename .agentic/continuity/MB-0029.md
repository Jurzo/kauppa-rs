## Dead ends and rejected approaches

- `hx-ext="response-targets"` on the forms themselves (RR-0018 as written, #58): htmx dispatches `htmx:beforeSwap` on the swap target and looks up extensions from there upward, so a form inside `<body>` never activates it. Markup-only tests can't catch this; P4 did.

- A listener script in `assets/` or `hx-on::before-swap`: forbidden (executable outside `assets/vendor/`; eval under MB-0031's CSP). htmx 2.0.x has no per-element `responseHandling` attribute.
