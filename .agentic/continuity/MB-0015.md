## Dead ends and rejected approaches

- Posting to `/lists/{id}/recipes` from the recipe page was rejected: the form action would have to change with the selected list, which needs JavaScript.
- Naming the handler `add_to_list` clashed with the brief's P2 test name (`#[post]` generates a unit struct of the handler's name), so the handler is `add_recipe_to_list`; the route path is the brief's.

## Decisions and constraints

- Decision: Drafted as an MB-0005 follow-up at the owner's request (2026-10-31). No schema change, so no RR is expected.
- Decision: The owner accepted the brief on 2026-11-06 by asking to continue work on the unimplemented briefs.
- Decision (`may_decide`, parsing): shared, not duplicated. Only `parse_multiplier` was needed and it moved nowhere; it became `pub` in `routes::lists`. The list-id parser (`parse_list`) is new and local to `routes::recipes`.
- Decision (`may_decide`, markup and placement): the section sits under the ingredient list with an `<h2>Add to a list</h2>`. The `<select>` starts on a "Choose a list" placeholder; after an add or a rejection the chosen list stays selected. Over htmx, the confirmation ("Added 3 items to Viikonloppu — open list", "1 item" when singular) replaces the section together with a fresh form, so another add needs no reload. It reuses the `add-product` form styles; `assets/app.css` is unchanged.
- Decision: fragment only for `HX-Request` without `HX-Boosted` (a local `wants_fragment` in `routes::recipes`, used only by the new handler); boosted and plain requests get the 303 to the list. Other recipe handlers are unchanged.
- Constraint: rejections render through the recipe editor, which loads the recipe owner-scoped first, so another user's recipe is a 404 even with otherwise bad input.
