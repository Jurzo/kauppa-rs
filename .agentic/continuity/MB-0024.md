## Dead ends and rejected approaches

- A handler attribute (`#[get("/assets/…")]`) outside `routes::protected`: the MB-0018 guard (rule 4) requires every handler attribute to be registered there. Builder calls (`web::resource`, `.default_service`, …) are banned by rule 1. Hence the `HttpServiceFactory`, as `Files` is.
- `crates/kauppa-web/src/assets.rs` at the crate root: needs `mod assets;` in `main.rs`, a trigger path. Placed in `routes/` instead (CRP-0011 sub-question 4).
- Asserting `/assets/app%2Ecss` is 404: actix-web's router decodes unreserved characters before matching, so it's the same URL as `/assets/app.css` and is served.
