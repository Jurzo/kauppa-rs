## Open questions and next steps

- Smallest next action: The owner reviews, then the branch is pushed and the PR opened with the drafted Merge-Readiness Pack.
- Known unknowns (for the owner to confirm, not escalated):
  - Page budget at 500 names: p95 is 3 ms, but the editor grows from 3.3 KB to 27.7 KB uncompressed, because the app has no compression middleware. That is about 0.2 s once per page load on a 1 Mbit/s 3G link. The agent judged this below the brief's "bloats noticeably" slow-mode trigger. The owner may disagree, and the alternative is the brief's htmx `<option>` search endpoint.
  - Sort order: `name_key` order is code-point order after lowercasing, so names beginning with Ä sort before Å, then Ö, all after z. That is not Finnish alphabetical order (Å, Ä, Ö). It matches the brief's plan and the store view's ordering.
- Where to gather evidence: the commits on the branch; the test names in the brief; the smoke log in the PR body.

## Dead ends and rejected approaches

- Rendering the datalist inside the `#ingredients` fragment: every htmx edit of an ingredient re-sent the whole catalog (about 24 KB at 500 names). It was moved to `show.hbs`, which matches the brief's "renders it once".
- Asserting in P2 that another user's page lacks "Maito": "e.g. Maito" is the input's placeholder. The test checks the other names instead.

## Decisions and constraints

- Owner (2026-11-06): the brief is accepted. The hints are display only, and `check_ingredient` stays the sole enforcement.
- `may_decide`:
  - The repo method is named `ProductRepo::suggestion_names`. It returns `Vec<String>` and includes uncategorized products, because they are still matches the hint explains.
  - The trigger is `input changed delay:300ms`, with `hx-target="#ingredient-match"` and `hx-swap="outerHTML"`. The hint is `<p id="ingredient-match" aria-live="polite">`, referenced by the input's `aria-describedby`.
  - The hint is a separate partial, `templates/recipes/_match_hint.hbs`. It is included in the add form and returned by the route.
- Not decided by the brief, decided here: without `HX-Request` (or with `HX-Boosted`), the match route 303s to the editor, following the actix-web pack's "or 303-redirect to the page".
- Constraints held: no migration, no dependency, no JavaScript file, no inline JS, and matching only through `ProductRepo::find_by_name` (`name_key`, RR-0003).
